## A note on the approach

1. **Two systems stack.** An adjective carries both its *degree* and its *agreement ending* at once. Separate the two and endings become mechanical.
2. **Case = the noun's job.** Find the case by asking what the noun is doing, or recognizing the preposition in front of it.
